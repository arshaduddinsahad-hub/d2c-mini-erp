# Claude Project Instructions

## Before Starting
1. Read docs/PROJECT_CONTEXT.md.
2. Read CURRENT_TASK.md to identify the active task.
3. Read HANDOFF.md if continuing work from another session.
4. Inspect only the relevant files and workflows.

## Working Rules
- Do not repeat completed work without a specific reason.
- Do not assume an implementation is correct without verification.
- Preserve existing architecture and business rules.
- Do not expose secrets or credentials.
- Do not activate or publish n8n workflows without explicit approval.
- Do not modify production data during tests without authorization.

## Before Ending a Session
Update CURRENT_TASK.md and HANDOFF.md with:
- What changed
- What was verified
- What remains incomplete
- The next concrete action
- Relevant files and workflow identifiers

Keep handoff notes concise and factual.
